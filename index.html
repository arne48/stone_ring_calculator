Calculation assumptions
  • Widths are treated as lengths along their corresponding circular reference edges.
  • With shims enabled, one repeating unit is one wedge followed by one shim; Snap modes always use equal whole counts of both.
  • The repeating-unit count is solved on the wedge-stone outer reference circle from wedge outer width plus shim width.
  • The desired inner diameter refers to the wedge-stone inner guide. A deeper outer-aligned shim can reduce the actual clear opening.
  • Stone height is ignored; this is a two-dimensional plan view.
